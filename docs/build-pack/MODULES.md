# PatchDub · module index

These are dependency-ordered work units. Once a contract is agreed, UI, adapter and test contributors may proceed on fixtures matching that contract and integrate against its real implementation before acceptance.

1. [M01 · Revision and subtitle import](prompts/M01.md) — Create workspace-scoped episode storage, bounded WebVTT parsing, original hashes and previous-target associations. Preserve parsed cue text and millisecond timestamps.
2. [M02 · Alignment and repair planning](prompts/M02.md) — Map old and new cues, record reasons and groups, distinguish retime, edit, insertion and deletion, and offer a manual disambiguation action.
3. [M03 · Scoped purchase and translation worker](prompts/M03.md) — Price the full approved repair window, reserve the cap atomically, dispatch through the chosen Kite-compatible seller and record the rail and independently observed receipt. Keep a local adapter for deterministic contract testing.
4. [M04 · Terminology and timing checks](prompts/M04.md) — Validate numbers, placeholders and glossary terms against the pinned revision. Check cue bounds, unintended overlap and display-time limits. Emit per-cue findings and source offsets.
5. [M05 · Reviewer workspace](prompts/M05.md) — Build accessible timeline navigation, editable target rows, reasoned resolve/reject actions, cost indicators and version-aware saves.
6. [M06 · Export and billable intervals](prompts/M06.md) — Serialize valid UTF-8 WebVTT, ordered cues and a manifest with parent hashes, findings and accepted usage. Calculate billable changed seconds as an interval union.
7. [M07 · Recovery and studio acceptance](prompts/M07.md) — Add an operator queue for unknown charges, artifact retention and a replayable studio demonstration through upload, review, purchase and export.
