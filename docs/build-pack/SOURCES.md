## Project lineage

Deliver an approved WebVTT revision with provenance, a cost ledger and a changed-cue report. The initial product promises subtitle localization. Rights-cleared source material and a native-language reviewer are prerequisites. Overlapping speech and timing changes can expand the repair window. Platform interfaces are documented in [provider notes](KITE-AND-PROVIDERS.md). Market demand, integration behavior, source data access and live payment authority require separate evidence.
